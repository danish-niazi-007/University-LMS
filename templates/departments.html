ACADEMIC DEPARTMENTS

Six departments. Six ways to specialize.

Every department runs its own labs, faculty, and curriculum — but all of them report to the same standard for teaching quality and graduate outcomes.

DEPARTMENT OF

Computer Science & IT

The largest department on campus, covering software engineering, AI/ML, and computer networks. Final-year students build real systems — including the university's own learning portal — rather than working from toy assignments.

The department runs a dedicated systems lab with GPU workstations, and its faculty publish actively in machine learning and distributed systems.

DEPARTMENT OF

Electrical Engineering

Focused on power systems and electronics, with coursework shaped around the region's own grid and renewable-energy needs rather than generic textbook cases.

Students get hands-on lab time from second year onward, working with real power electronics and control systems equipment.

DEPARTMENT OF

Business & Management

Covers accounting, finance, and entrepreneurship, taught through case studies drawn from actual businesses in Mianwali and the wider Punjab region.

The department runs a student-led business incubator, giving BBA and MBA students a place to test real ideas before graduating.

Civil Engineering

Trains students in structures and water resource management — directly relevant to a district shaped by the Indus river and the Thal canal system.

Field visits to local irrigation and construction sites are built into the curriculum starting in the third year.
