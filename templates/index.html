University of Mianwali was founded to keep talent in the region it comes from — so students from the Thal and the Indus belt don't have to leave home to get a serious education.

Today the university runs undergraduate and graduate programs across engineering, computer science, business, and the humanities, with a teaching model built around small sections, applied labs, and faculty who publish as actively as they teach.

Every department is judged on one question: does a graduate leave more capable than when they arrived. That's the standard the campus is built around.

HOW THE UNIVERSITY IS ORGANIZED

Six departments, one standard.

Each department sets its own curriculum but answers to the same bar for research output, lab access, and graduate outcomes.

01

Computer Science & IT

Software engineering, AI/ML, and networks, with a dedicated systems lab and an active LMS built in-house.
02

Electrical Engineering

Power systems and electronics, with a focus on the region's grid and renewable-energy needs.
03

Business & Management

Accounting, finance, and entrepreneurship, taught alongside real local business case studies.
04

Civil Engineering

Structures and water resources — directly relevant to a district shaped by the Indus and the Thal canal system.
05

English & Humanities

Language, literature, and communication skills that carry across every other degree on campus.
06

Mathematics & Sciences

The foundational department every engineering and CS student passes through in year one.
